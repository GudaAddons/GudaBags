# GudaBags

GudaBags is a lightweight, all-in-one bag replacement for World of Warcraft. It puts all
your bags into one clean window with smart categories, one-click sorting, search and
filters, item protection, and cross-character tracking.

It works on **Classic Era**, **TBC**, **MoP Classic**, and **Retail**.

![GudaBags](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1449496/description_53d309c9-7e9f-48c0-a73a-31ed5b3ec4db.png)

---

## Key Features

### Three View Modes

- **Category View:** Items are sorted into smart groups like Equipment, Consumables,
  Trade Goods, and Quest Items. Click any category to collapse or expand it.
- **Single View:** A classic flat grid. All your items in one clean window, like the
  default bags but better.
- **Split View:** Your bags shown as separate columns side by side. You can set how many
  columns to show and choose which bags get full width rows.

Switch between views at any time in the settings. Bag and bank views are set separately.

### Smart Sorting

One click to organize everything:

- Groups items by type, subtype, quality, and name
- Restacks partial stacks to free up space
- Smooth sorting with no freezing and no UI lockup
- Optional right to left and reverse stack sorting
- On Retail you can choose between Blizzard's native sort and the GudaBags sort engine

### Category System

22 built-in categories that you can fully customize:

- Recent Items, Quest, Junk, BoE, Warbound, Weapons, Armor, Consumables, Trade Goods,
  and more
- **Recent Items:** A special category for items you picked up recently. You can set how
  long items stay there, from 1 to 60 minutes.
- **Category Groups:** Categories are organized into groups (Main, Other, Class) with
  headers you can collapse.
- **Merge Groups:** Combine all categories in a group into one section for a cleaner look.
- **Custom Categories:** Make your own using 16 rule types, such as item type, name
  pattern, quality, and tooltip text.
- **Drag and drop:** Reorder categories and move them between groups.
- **Group Identical Items:** Stack identical items visually in category view.

### Search and Filtering

- **Search Bar:** Start typing to find items by name. Only matching items are shown.
- **Filter Chips:** Quick filter buttons under the search bar. Filter by quality, item
  type, or special tags like BoE and Recently Picked Up.

## Quick Access Bars

Two floating bars that stay visible outside the bag window.

### Quest Item Bar

![Quest Item Bar](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_937c677d-ae64-4d52-aadd-567d1d9c4de4.png)

All your usable quest items in one place, so you do not have to dig through your bags
in the middle of a quest.

- Click to use items directly
- Hover to show more quest items
- Hides itself in battlegrounds (you can turn this off)
- Adjustable icon size and column count

### Tracked Item Bar

![Tracked Item Bar](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_9701901b-e36c-4296-94e1-633c096184cc.png)

Pin any item you want to watch. Good for farming herbs, ore, cloth, or reputation items.

- **Ctrl + Alt + Click** any item to start or stop tracking it
- Shows the total count from all your bags
- Your tracked items are saved between sessions

## Mailbox Viewer

![Mailbox](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1449496/description_4476bdcd-ea46-4f2b-8cb3-213142d7bc3f.gif)

## Bulk Mail Sending

Send every copy of an item to another character with one click.

Open a mailbox, type the name of the person you want to send to, then
**Shift + Right Click** an item in your bags. GudaBags finds every copy of that item in
your bags and attaches them. When a mail is full with 12 attachments, it sends that mail
and starts a new one. It keeps going until there are no copies left.

Sending 40 stacks of cloth to your bank alt used to take 40 clicks and 4 separate mails.
Now it takes one click.

- **Confirmation first:** Before anything is sent, a window shows you how many items will
  go, how many mails it will take, and how much the postage will cost.
- **Attach only mode:** If you leave the recipient field empty, the addon only attaches
  the items and stops. You can then type a name and send the mail yourself.
- **Protected items stay in your bags:** Locked items and equipment set items are never
  sent. Soulbound items and anything else that cannot be mailed are skipped, and the
  addon tells you how many were skipped.
- **Safe to stop:** If you walk away from the mailbox or enter combat, the addon stops
  and tells you how many mails it sent. It also refuses to start if you have a COD amount
  in the mail, or if you do not have enough money for the postage.

You can turn this feature off in the settings, on the General tab under Automation.

## Cross-Character Tracking

- **Tooltip item counts:** Hover over any item to see how many you have across all
  characters on the realm, split by bags, bank, mail, and equipped
- **Warband Bank counts:** On Retail, tooltip counts also include your Warband Bank
- **Offline character viewing:** Browse the bags, bank, and mail of other characters
  without logging in
- **Total gold display:** See your combined gold across all characters in the footer
- **Currency display:** The currencies for your expansion are shown in the footer

---

## Item Protection

- **Item Locking:** Lock any item so it cannot be sold, deleted, or disenchanted by
  accident. Locked items show a lock icon and are protected at merchants.
- **Equipment Set Protection:** Items in an equipment set are protected from being sold.
  You can turn this on or off in the settings.

---

## Automation

- **Auto Open and Close Bags:** Bags open when you visit a merchant, mailbox, auction
  house, bank, or trade window, and close when you leave. Both can be turned on or off
  separately.
- **Auto Sell Junk:** Gray items are sold when you visit a merchant. Locked items and
  equipment set items are always protected.
- **Auto Repair:** Your gear is repaired when you visit a merchant that can repair. This
  is off by default.
- **Bulk Mail Attach:** Shift + Right Click an item at a mailbox to send every copy of it
  to another character. This is on by default.

## Customization

Open the settings with `/gb config` or click the gear icon.

![Settings](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_5d5a8ad5-1365-4e0c-9817-cd04fe7aa50d.png)

### Appearance

- **Two themes:** Guda (dark and minimal) and Blizzard (native WoW look)
- Icon size (22 to 64px), font size, and spacing
- Background opacity (0 to 100%)
- Window locking so you do not move the window by accident

### Item Display

- Quality color borders, with separate toggles for equipment and other items
- Unusable item marking
- Junk graying, with an option to include white equippable items
- Equipment set indicators
- Item level on icons (Retail)

### Layout

- Configurable columns: 5 to 22 for bags, 5 to 36 for bank, 10 to 36 for guild bank
- Show or hide the search bar, filter chips, footer, and category item counts
- Group identical items in category view
- Equipment sets shown as named categories

### Profiles

- Save your current settings as a named profile
- Load, delete, or reset profiles at any time
- Import and export profiles to share with friends or move between characters

## Multi-Expansion Support

GudaBags detects your WoW version and turns on the right features.

| Expansion | Special Features |
|-----------|------------------|
| Classic Era | Keyring, quiver and ammo bags, soul bags |
| TBC Classic | Keyring, quiver and ammo bags, guild bank |
| MoP Classic | Gem bags, inscription bags, guild bank, currency display |
| Retail (TWW) | Reagent bag, character and warband bank tabs, item level display, currency display, native sorting |

---

## Equipment Set Integration

Works with several equipment set addons:

- **Blizzard Equipment Manager** (Retail and Wrath or later)
- **ItemRack** (Classic and TBC)
- **Outfitter**

Equipment set items are marked visually, and each set can appear as its own category in
category view.

## Slash Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| `/gb` or `/guda` | Toggle bags |
| `/gb sort` | Sort your bags |
| `/gb bank` | Toggle bank (when at a banker) |
| `/gb config` | Open settings |
| `/gb chars` | List saved characters |
| `/gb count <itemID>` | Count an item across all characters |
| `/gb save` | Force save bags to the database |
| `/gb status` | Show the detected expansion and enabled features |
| `/gb help` | Show all commands |

---

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
|----------|--------------|
| **Ctrl + Alt + Click** | Track or untrack an item on the Tracked Item Bar |
| **Ctrl + Right Click** | Lock or unlock an item so it cannot be sold |
| **Alt + Right Click** | Pin or unpin a bag slot so sorting does not move it |
| **Shift + Right Click** | At a mailbox, send every copy of the item |

## Installation

### From CurseForge

1. Use the CurseForge app to install it automatically
2. Or download and extract it to `World of Warcraft/_anniversary_/Interface/AddOns/`

### Manual Install

1. Download the latest release
2. Extract the `GudaBags` folder into your AddOns directory
3. Restart WoW or type `/reload`
